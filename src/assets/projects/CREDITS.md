# Sample project charts

Both charts come from Kokou Hugues Minasseh's MSc projects (University of Hull) and are
used on the site as sample projects.

- `road-accidents-heatmap.png`: `reports/figures/temporal_heatmap.png` in
  https://github.com/Hugues-hash/msc-traffic-accident-data-mining. Built from the UK
  Department for Transport STATS19 data for 2018, under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
- `churn-confusion-matrices.png`: `reports/figures/churn_confusion_matrices.png` in
  https://github.com/Hugues-hash/msc-customer-behaviour-analysis.

To swap a chart, replace the file with one of the same name, or change the import at the top
of `src/content/site-content.ts`.
